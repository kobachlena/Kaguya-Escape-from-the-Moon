# Kaguya-Escape-from-the-Moon
Kaguya: Escape from the Moon is an (almost) endless runner mobile game where players take on the role of Kaguya, a princess who has built a road from the Moon to Earth. However, her parents, who don't want her to leave, have placed torii (red Japanese gates) along the road to stop her. The ultimate goal is to reach Earth, which is 363,104 km away.
